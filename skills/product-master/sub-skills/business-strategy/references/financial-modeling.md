# Financial Modeling & Unit Economics

Master the math of business growth and sustainability.

## 💰 Core Metrics
- **CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)**: The total revenue a business can expect from a single customer account.
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: The total cost of sales and marketing efforts to acquire a new customer.
- **LTV/CAC Ratio**: Aim for > 3:1 for a healthy SaaS business.
- **Payback Period**: Time required to recover the CAC.

## 📉 Churn & Retention
- **Logo Churn**: Percentage of customers who leave.
- **Revenue Churn**: Percentage of MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) lost.
- **Negative Churn**: When expansion revenue from existing customers exceeds lost revenue from churned customers (The Holy Grail of SaaS).

## 🧪 Scenario Planning
- **Best Case / Base Case / Worst Case**: Always model three scenarios when projecting revenue or costs.
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: Identify which variables (e.g., pricing, conversion rate) have the biggest impact on the bottom line.

## 🚀 ROI Calculation
- `ROI = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) * 100`
- Always include "Opportunity Cost" in your strategic ROI assessments.
