# KPI & OKR Frameworks

Align organizational goals with technical execution using structured metrics.

## 🌟 The North Star Metric
Identify the single most important metric that reflects the core value your product delivers to customers.
- **Example (SaaS)**: "Number of active monthly users performing [core action]."
- **Example (E-commerce)**: "Repeat purchase rate."

## 🎯 OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)
- **Objectives**: Qualitative, ambitious, and time-bound goals (e.g., "Become the market leader in Search AI").
- **Key Results**: Quantitative, measurable outcomes that indicate success (e.g., "Reduce search latency by 40%").

## 📊 KPI Hierarchy
1. **L0: Executive Metrics**: Revenue, Burn Rate, Total Active Customers.
2. **L1: Departmental Metrics**: Conversion Rate, Churn Rate, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
3. **L2: Technical Metrics**: System Uptime, Response Time, Error Rate.

## 📋 Best Practices
- **Avoid Vanity Metrics**: Total Registered Users is a vanity metric; Active Daily Users is an actionable metric.
- **Balanced Scorecard**: Measure Financial, Customer, Internal Process, and Learning/Growth metrics simultaneously.
- **Benchmarking**: Compare your KPIs against industry standards to identify gaps.
