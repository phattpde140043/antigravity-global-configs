# Apple-Level Design Principles (The 10 Commandments)

Inspired by Jony Ive and Dieter Rams, these principles ensure a premium, timeless product experience.

## 1. Radical Simplicity
Remove everything that is not essential. Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.

## 2. Material Honesty
Every element exists for a reason. Don't hide the true nature of the materials or components used.

## 3. Less is More
Restraint is a design decision. Do not add features just because you can.

## 4. Systemic Coherence
Everything is part of a single, unified system. Consistency creates trust.

## 5. Details Matter
The user feels the details even if they don't consciously notice them (e.g., corner radius, micro-animations).

## 6. Function Defines Form
Aesthetics must serve the purpose. Beautiful design that doesn't work is a failure.

## 7. Durability
Design that ages well. Avoid "trendy" patterns that become obsolete in a year.

## 8. Accessibility as Standard
Accessibility is not a feature; it is the default starting point.

## 9. Continuity Across Screens
A unified experience whether the user is on mobile, desktop, or tablet.

## 10. Delightful Surprise
Add unexpected moments of joy (the "Aha!" moment) that enchant the user.
