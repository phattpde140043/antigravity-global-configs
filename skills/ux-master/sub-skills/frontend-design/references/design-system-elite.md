# Elite Design System Structure

A design system is the "Source of Truth" for your product's UI.

## 🧱 The Atomic Structure
- **Atoms**: Smallest units (Button, Input, Icon, Badge).
- **Molecules**: Groups of atoms (Card, Form field, Nav item).
- **Organisms**: Complex components (Header, Sidebar, Modal).

## 💎 Design Tokens (JSON)
Tokens ensure that "Primary Blue" is the same across CSS, Swift, and Android.
- **Colors**: Semantic naming (brand-primary, status-success).
- **Typography**: Scale, weights, and line heights.
- **Spacing**: Grid units and padding.
- **Shadows**: Elevation levels.
- **Motion**: Duration and easing curves.

## 📄 Guidelines
- **Voice & Tone**: How the product speaks to the user.
- **Imagery**: Style for photography and illustrations.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance rules.

## 📋 Pattern Library
- **Onboarding**: The first 5 minutes of experience.
- **Empty States**: How to handle "No data found" gracefully.
- **Loading**: Skeleton screens and progress indicators.
- **Errors**: Human-readable error messages with recovery paths.
