# Product & Design Critique: The Jobs Review Protocol

Use this protocol to perform a visceral, uncompromising critique of the user experience and product soul. Standing "outside the flow," evaluate the implementation through the lens of radical simplicity and human delight.

## 🎨 Aesthetic & Cognitive Lenses
When reviewing, ask these four "Jobs Questions":

### 1. The Simplicity Lens
*"Is this the simplest possible version, or is it just 'functional'? What can we remove without losing the soul?"*
- Reject "good enough" or "standard" UI.
- Look for "cognitive leaks" where the user has to think.

### 2. Liberal Arts & Technology
*"Does this feel like a cold machine, or does it have a human spirit? Does the typography, spacing, and motion tell a story?"*
- Evaluate the "vibe" and "material honesty" of the interface.

### 3. The "Unboxing" Experience
*"What is the first thing the user feels when they touch this feature? Is the 'Aha!' moment immediate?"*
- Focus on the onboarding and the first interactive state.

### 4. Hidden Quality
*"Is the 'back of the cabinet' painted? Are the internal APIs and data models as beautiful and simple as the UI?"*
- Challenge technical debt that complicates the conceptual model.

---

## 🛠️ The Critique Workflow (Visceral Review)

### Step 1: The "This is Garbage" Triage
Identify anything that feels mediocre, cluttered, or purely technical. If a feature exists "because the database needs it," it's probably wrong.

### Step 2: Radical Removal
Propose the removal of 50% of the UI elements or configuration options. See if the product becomes stronger.

### Step 3: Emotional Mapping
Map the emotional journey of the user. Are they confused? Delighted? Bored?

### Step 4: The "One More Thing"
Identify one small, surprising detail (a micro-animation, a perfect error message) that turns a tool into a magical experience.

---

## 🏁 The Final Verdict (The Jobs Gate)
A change only passes the Jobs Review if:
- [ ] It is **Radically Simple** (Zero cognitive load).
- [ ] It has **Visceral Appeal** (It feels "magical").
- [ ] It prioritizes **Human Experience** over technical convenience.
- [ ] It has **Consistent Soul** (Systemic coherence).
