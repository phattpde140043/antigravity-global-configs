# Investor Outreach

Write investor communication that is personalized, concise, and easy to act on.

---

## Purpose

Use this skill for outreach messages across fundraising stages: first touch, follow-up, intro request, and process updates.

---

## When to Activate

- writing cold outreach to investors
- drafting warm intro requests and forwardable blurbs
- sending follow-ups after no response or after meetings
- sending process updates during fundraising
- tailoring messages to fund thesis and partner fit

---

## Core Rules

1. Personalize every outbound message.
2. Keep the ask low-friction and explicit.
3. Use proof points, not adjectives.
4. Keep copy tight.
5. Never send text that could fit any investor.

---

## Scope Boundaries

Use this skill for:
- investor communication structure and sequencing
- personalization strategy and concise asks
- follow-up cadence and process updates

Do NOT use this skill as primary source for:
- building core fundraising assets and financial logic
- voice profiling methodology
- legal guidance on fundraising terms

Delegation:
- use `investor-materials` for deck/memo/model consistency
- use `brand-voice` when user wants strict personal style matching

---

## Voice Handling

If voice consistency matters, run `brand-voice` first and reuse the VOICE PROFILE.
This skill focuses on investor-specific structure and ask discipline.

---

## Hard Bans

Delete and rewrite:
- I would love to connect
- excited to share
- generic thesis praise with no specific tie-in
- vague founder adjectives
- begging language
- soft closing questions when direct ask is clearer

---

## Message Playbooks

## Cold Email Structure

1. subject: short and specific
2. opener: why this investor specifically
3. pitch: what, why now, and strongest proof
4. ask: one concrete next step
5. sign-off: name, role, and one credibility anchor if needed

## Warm Intro Request

- explain fit in one to two lines
- include forwardable blurb under 100 words
- make intro ask easy to forward without edits

## Follow-Up Cadence (Default)

- day 0: initial outbound
- day 4 to 5: short follow-up with one new datapoint
- day 10 to 12: final follow-up with clean close

Do not continue nudging unless user wants an extended sequence.

## Post-Meeting Update

Include:
- what was discussed
- promised follow-up and answer
- one fresh proof point when available
- explicit next step and owner

---

## Personalization Sources

Use one or more:
- relevant portfolio context
- public thesis, talk, or published view
- mutual connection
- concrete fit with investor focus

If context is missing, state that personalization is still required.

---

## Quality Gate

Before delivery:

- [ ] message is genuinely personalized
- [ ] ask is explicit and low-friction
- [ ] proof point is concrete and current
- [ ] filler praise and softeners removed
- [ ] word count is tight and skimmable

---

## Output Contract

When activated, return:

1. target investor profile and personalization anchor
2. primary message draft
3. optional follow-up sequence
4. forwardable intro blurb (if requested)
5. personalization gaps still needed before send
