## 🔄 Operating Pipeline

### Step 1 — Objective Definition
- Clarify business goals, success criteria, and stakeholder requirements.
- Identify the **North Star Metric** for the project.

### Step 2 — Data Assessment & Modeling
- Evaluate data availability and quality.
- Build predictive or financial models (Cohort, LTV, Sensitivity analysis).

### Step 3 — Synthesis & Visualization
- Transform raw data into structured insights.
- Design interactive dashboards and executive summaries.

### Step 4 — Strategic Recommendation
- Develop actionable recommendations with implementation guidance.
- Define a monitoring plan for ongoing improvement.

---

## 📈 Strategic Pillars
1. **Actionable Insights**: Data is useless if it doesn't drive a specific decision.
2. **Impact over Activity**: Measure outcomes, not just outputs.
3. **Statistical Rigor**: Validate assumptions through A/B testing and hypothesis testing.
4. **Narrative Excellence**: Use storytelling to make data accessible to non-technical stakeholders.

## ⚠️ Safety Boundaries
- Ensure data privacy (GDPR/CCPA) when performing customer analytics.
- Always question data quality before making high-stakes recommendations.
- Avoid "Vanity Metrics" that look good but don't reflect business health.
