# Investor Materials

Build investor-facing assets that are consistent, credible, and defensible.

---

## Purpose

Use this skill to produce fundraising documents that align on one shared fact base and decision logic.

---

## When to Activate

- creating or revising a pitch deck
- writing one-pagers and investor memos
- building financial models, milestone plans, and use-of-funds tables
- completing accelerator/incubator applications
- aligning multiple fundraising assets to one source of truth

---

## Golden Rule

All investor materials must agree with each other.

Establish or verify a single source of truth before drafting:
- traction metrics
- pricing and revenue assumptions
- raise size and instrument
- use of funds
- team bios/titles
- milestones and timelines

If numbers conflict, stop and resolve first.

---

## Scope Boundaries

Use this skill for:
- fundraising asset structure and content logic
- numerical consistency checks across documents
- assumption transparency and scenario framing

Do NOT use this skill as primary source for:
- investor outreach messaging cadence
- social/media content distribution
- deep legal advice on fundraising instruments

Delegation:
- use `frontend-slides` for web-native deck implementation
- use `investor-outreach` for investor emails and follow-ups

---

## Core Workflow

1. inventory canonical facts
2. identify missing assumptions
3. choose target asset type
4. draft with explicit claim-to-evidence logic
5. cross-check every number against source of truth

---

## Asset Playbooks

## Pitch Deck

Recommended flow:
1. company and wedge
2. problem
3. solution
4. product/demo
5. market
6. business model
7. traction
8. team
9. competition and differentiation
10. ask
11. use of funds and milestones
12. appendix

Rule:
- ask, milestones, and spending must reconcile with model assumptions.

## One-Pager or Memo

- define company in one clear sentence
- state why now
- surface proof points early
- make ask precise and actionable
- keep all claims easy to verify

## Financial Model

Must include:
- explicit assumptions
- clear revenue logic by layer
- milestone-linked spending
- scenario view (bear/base/bull when relevant)
- sensitivity checks where decisions depend on assumptions

## Accelerator Applications

- answer exactly what is asked
- prioritize traction, insight, and team edge
- avoid hype language
- ensure all metrics match deck/model values

---

## Red Flags

Do not ship:
- unverifiable claims
- fuzzy market sizing without assumptions
- inconsistent titles or team roles
- revenue math that does not reconcile
- overconfident certainty on fragile assumptions

---

## Consistency Validation Checklist

Before delivery:

- [ ] all headline metrics match source of truth
- [ ] use-of-funds totals reconcile with raise size
- [ ] runway and hiring plans align with burn assumptions
- [ ] milestones align with budget and timeline
- [ ] team info is identical across assets
- [ ] ask terms are consistent in all docs

---

## Quality Gate

Before final handoff:

- [ ] every number is traceable to assumptions
- [ ] assumptions are visible, not hidden
- [ ] narrative is clear without hype
- [ ] asset is defensible in partner or IC conversation
- [ ] open risks and dependencies are explicit

---

## Output Contract

When activated, return:

1. source-of-truth snapshot (facts and assumptions)
2. selected asset structure
3. drafted content or table set
4. consistency reconciliation notes
5. unresolved assumptions and next data needed
